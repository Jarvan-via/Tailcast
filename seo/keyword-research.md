# Keyword and SERP Research

Research date: 2026-08-24

Market: English-language discovery first, with French/Portuguese/Spanish localization opportunities noted separately. The live search interface was queried for every keyword in the table. A direct raw Google UI replay was attempted but Google returned its safeguard page, and the connected browser session could not complete a reliable capture. Therefore competitor presence and intent are evidence-backed, but exact Google positions and search volume are not claimed. Validate positions and impressions in Search Console after publication.

## Business Value Score

The score is out of 100:

- search/commercial intent: 0–30;
- target-seller fit: 0–25;
- verified Autopricy support: 0–25;
- conversion likelihood: 0–10;
- competition opportunity: 0–10, where a weaker SERP scores higher.

Search volume is deliberately not included. Competition is a qualitative SERP assessment (`low`, `medium`, `high`) based on the number and specificity of relevant commercial pages observed—not an Ahrefs/SEMrush metric.

## Main findings

1. **Mirakl is the clearest whitespace.** Search results confuse operator software, connectors, ERP-style suites, and actual seller repricing. A precise seller-focused page can own the definition while honestly limiting support to assessed operators.
2. **Worten has strong product-market fit and manageable competition.** Existing pages from Boardfy and Boostmyshop are broad and sometimes templated. Autopricy can win on marketplace-specific price channels, shipping-inclusive competition, min/max boundaries, and submission-versus-confirmation evidence.
3. **FNAC/Darty require multilingual intent handling.** English commercial terms collide with French `reprise` (trade-in/recycling) results. Titles, H1s, and copy should include `Marketplace seller` and `repricing software`; a later French version should use `repricing marketplace`, not rely on `repricer` alone.
4. **OnBuy is high value but not easy.** Re-Pricer has already built an exact-match content cluster. Autopricy should differentiate with delayed platform confirmation, winning checks, raise-after-winning boundaries, and multi-marketplace management rather than generic “AI repricing.”
5. **Cdiscount needs differentiation from the native floor-price mechanic.** The page must explain package/feedback states, bulk rules, read-back, and multi-shop control. It should not pretend that “automatic lowest price” is unique.
6. **Mercado Libre is not a P0 landing page.** Official free automation dominates the SERP, while Autopricy’s production enablement and Catalog eligibility remain conditional.
7. **No search-volume claims are justified yet.** Search Console impressions after launch are the first reliable zero-party demand signal; paid keyword tools can be added later if useful.
