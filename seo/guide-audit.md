# Phase 3 Guide Editorial Audit

Date: 2026-08-24

## Scope

The first five English guides target high-business-value seller questions for Mirakl, Worten, OnBuy, FNAC/Darty and Cdiscount. They are educational companions to the commercial landing pages, not duplicated templates.

## Claim boundaries

- Mirakl support is described per selected operator; no universal compatibility claim.
- Worten price is presented as one Winning Offer input alongside the marketplace's published shipping and seller-quality context; no Buy Box guarantee.
- OnBuy public material reviewed does not expose a complete fixed Winning Offer formula, so none is asserted.
- FNAC and Darty share a verified XML integration boundary while store identity and competitive logic remain marketplace-specific.
- Cdiscount package submission, item feedback and later Offer read-back are distinct evidence states.
- Examples are explicitly illustrative and contain no customer, performance or revenue claims.

## SEO and editorial checks

- One descriptive H1 and at least seven H2 sections per guide.
- Unique title and meta description, canonical, index/follow and English hreflang.
- Article, BreadcrumbList and visible/matching FAQPage structured data.
- Direct guide-to-platform link near the start plus relevant feature links.
- Primary marketplace or developer documentation is linked in every guide.
- Generic AI openings, inflated adjectives, invented quotes and guaranteed placement claims are absent.
- Copy was reviewed for sentence variety, specificity and unsupported certainty under the local `writer` workflow.

## Publishing gate

Run the deterministic SEO check, inspect desktop and mobile rendering, verify production HTTP/canonical/sitemap behavior, then resubmit the sitemap. Backlink submissions remain gated until these checks pass.
