## Evidence and decisions

1. **eCommerce Tech** is the strongest specialist target. It has a Product & Price Management category with repricing competitors and describes an editorial software directory used by ecommerce merchants. The final submission route and any cost must be checked before form entry.
2. **Capterra, Software Advice and GetApp** are all part of G2 Digital Markets. They have genuine software-buyer audiences and profile governance, but should be handled as one coordinated onboarding effort rather than three independent submissions.
3. **G2** accepts B2B software, verifies eligibility and category, and separates product submission from ownership claim. Both actions require independent authorization.
4. **SourceForge** offers an unpaid basic business-software listing and human category review. Its form requests name, email, title, phone, founding year and location, so verified master data and action-time authorization are mandatory.
5. **SaaSHub** has relevant ecommerce and price-comparison categories plus an approval process. Its separate feature for submitting to 100+ directories is outside this project and must not be used.
6. **Product Hunt** is valuable for product discovery but is a launch/community publication, not a normal directory form. It needs a separate launch brief, personal maker account, images and publication authorization.
7. **SellerTrove and EcomAI** fail product-fit gates. Autopricy must not be described as an AI tool merely to obtain a listing.

## Duplicate checks

Public site-restricted searches on 2026-08-25 found no Autopricy product page for Capterra, G2, SourceForge, Product Hunt, SaaSHub, eCommerce Tech, SellerTrove or EcomAI.
