### Short tagline

Rule-based repricing for cross-border sellers on selected European marketplaces.

### Short description

Autopricy helps cross-border marketplace sellers monitor available competing Offers and automate eligible price changes inside seller-defined minimum and maximum boundaries. It keeps store identity, marketplace submission, processing and confirmed results visible as separate operational states.

### Full description

Autopricy is multi-marketplace repricing software for cross-border ecommerce sellers. It supports bounded automatic repricing, competitive Offer monitoring, minimum and maximum price rules, multi-store operations and execution history across Worten, FNAC, Darty, OnBuy, Cdiscount and selected Mirakl-powered marketplaces. Each marketplace uses its own verified data and update workflow; Autopricy does not present one generic connector as universal support. The product focuses on pricing and adjacent Offer operations rather than order, warehouse, procurement, finance or logistics ERP functions.
